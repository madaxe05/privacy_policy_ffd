Diamond Quest Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026 · Android package com.madaxe.sohan.freediamonds

Unofficial app. Diamond Quest is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by or approved by Garena. Free Fire and Diamonds are trademarks or property of their respective owners.

1. What the app does

You play mini-games, finish missions and claim daily rewards to earn in-app Coins. You can then request a reward from the Redeem tab, delivered as a Google Play redeem code. Coins are an in-app score. They are not money and have no cash value.

2. Information we collect

  • Account identifiers. A Firebase Authentication user ID. You start as an anonymous guest. If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID, name, email address and profile photo URL from Google.
  • Profile. Display name, nickname, chosen avatar and optional in-game username you type in.
  • Game and reward data. Coins, missions, scores, streaks, level, leaderboard position, redemption requests and their status.
  • Device and usage data. App version, device model, Android version, crash reports, performance data, an app installation identifier and general analytics events (for example, which screens are used).
  • Push notification token if you allow notifications.
  • Advertising data. See section 4.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone, and we never ask for your Free Fire or Garena password.

3. How we use it

  • To run the app: sign-in, saving progress, leaderboards and reward requests.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to verify that rewards from ads were genuinely earned.
  • To fix crashes, measure performance and improve the app.
  • To send reminders and reward updates if you allow notifications.
  • To show ads that fund the app.

4. Ads and your consent choices

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob (rewarded, interstitial and app-open ads). AdMob and its partners may use your device advertising ID and similar identifiers to serve and measure ads, and to limit how often you see them.

  • Where required (for example, in the EEA, the UK and some other regions), the app first shows Google's consent form, built on the IAB TCF standard through Google's User Messaging Platform. You can accept, decline or manage your choices. Ads are only requested as your consent status allows.
  • If the form applies to you, you can reopen it at any time from the privacy options entry on the Profile screen.
  • You can reset or opt out of your Android advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Learn more: how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

A reward for watching an ad is granted only after the ad is completed and confirmed by Google's server-side verification. Ads may be unavailable at any time.

5. Service providers

We use Google services to operate the app: Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Remote Config, Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, Cloud Messaging, App Check (Play Integrity), Google Sign-In and AdMob. These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies: policies.google.com/privacy and firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Sharing

We share data only with the providers above, when the law requires it, or to protect the app and its users from fraud or abuse. Your display name, nickname and avatar may be visible to other players on leaderboards. Your email address is never shown to other players.

7. Storage and retention

Progress is stored in Google Cloud (Firebase) infrastructure. Some preferences (such as theme and language) are stored on your device only. We keep account data while your account exists. Redemption and fraud-prevention records may be kept for a limited period after deletion where needed to prevent abuse or meet legal obligations.

8. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Delete your account and data: use Delete Account on the Profile screen. It removes your profile and progress from our servers.
  • Notifications: turn them off in the app or in Android settings.
  • Ads: use the privacy options entry and your Android advertising ID settings described above.
  • Uninstalling removes data stored on your device.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, and to object to or restrict some processing. To exercise a right, use the in-app options or contact us below.

9. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We use Firebase security rules, App Check and server-side checks for rewards. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/madaxe05/privacy_policy_ffd or use the Support page inside the app.
